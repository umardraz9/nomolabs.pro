A little help for the journey

Can I play without an internet connection?

The single-player adventure and local saves do not need a multiplayer server. Advertising requires internet access and an available ad.

How do I give arrows to Mara?

Open Bag, then Equipment & village trader, then Loadout. Move near a living Mara and choose “Give Mara 5 arrows.” You need five normal arrows and space in her 30-arrow quiver.

What if my arrows run out?

Recover the bundles dropped by defeated archers, craft normal arrows using parts, or fight in melee. When available, an optional rewarded ad can provide 25 normal arrows.

How does village trading work?

Complete a chapter and reach its village to receive delivery earnings once. Visit the trader to buy arrows and equipment with earned Gold and Silver. One Gold equals 100 Silver.

How do I keep my progress?

Use the game’s save and return options. Install updates over the existing app. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove your local journey; there is no developer cloud-save service.

Why is an ad unavailable?

Availability depends on connectivity, privacy setup and the ad service. No ad is guaranteed to be available. You can continue using the normal in-game supply and combat options.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include the chapter, what happened, the game version, device model and Android version. A screenshot and clear reproduction steps help. Do not include passwords, verification codes or bank information.

For privacy questions, read the Privacy Policy. Game-use information is in the Terms of Use.
