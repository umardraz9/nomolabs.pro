Keep your people
together.

The wagon carries more than supplies. Protect the horse, watch over your companions and choose how to prepare for the dangers ahead.

I / Defend

Fight with purpose.

Loose an arrow, dodge a heavy strike and find the opening behind a villain’s defenses. Share arrows with Mara so she can cover you at range.

II / Rescue

Leave no one behind.

Help stranded travelers, free trapped workers and restore the crossings that stand between your caravan and shelter.
