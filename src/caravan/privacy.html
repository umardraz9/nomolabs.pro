Privacy Policy

How game information, advertising and support are handled.

Effective date: 6 November 2026
App: The Last Caravan
Android package: com.lastcaravan.singleplayer
Developer: NomoLabs
Privacy contact: support@nomolabs.pro

About this policy

This policy explains how NomoLabs handles information when you play The Last Caravan, visit its website, or contact support. The game is a single-player adventure intended for players aged 13 and older.

Game progress and settings

Your journey, inventory, equipment, advertising reward receipts and settings are saved locally on your device. NomoLabs does not receive your saved game. The current game has no in-game account, developer-operated cloud saves, multiplayer, voice chat, or real-money in-app purchases. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game can remove your progress.

Advertising and information handled by Google

The Android game integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When advertising services run, Google may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and advertising interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account-related identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. These services use information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and security. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

NomoLabs does not operate a separate gameplay analytics service. Advertising SDK collection is separate from local game saves. Whether an ad is personalized and which identifiers are used depends on applicable law, your privacy choices, device settings and the ad service's configuration. Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps and sites, and Google's SDK data disclosure for more information.

Advertising choices

At first advertising setup, you can choose Under 16, 16 or older, or Keep ads off. Only the selected age range is saved locally, without a date of birth. Advertising services are not initialized for Under 16 or Keep ads off. Players aged 16 or older can open Settings → Display → Ad privacy choices for applicable consent options. Clearing app data removes the saved choice along with local progress. Where required, the game requests privacy choices before initializing advertising. If the required setup cannot be completed and no valid previous choice is available, ads remain unavailable while the game remains playable. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. Available choices and their effects vary by location and device.

Rewarded ads are optional. An eligible completed arrow-reward ad grants 25 normal arrows. Crafting, supplies recovered from defeated archers and melee combat provide alternatives. Occasional interstitial ads are limited to chapter transitions; an ad is not guaranteed to be available.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond, investigate problems and maintain a record of the request. Please send only the information needed for support, such as game version, device model and reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or identity documents. Email providers process messages to deliver and maintain correspondence.

Website services

The website is hosted on Vercel. The hosting service may process technical request details such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs to deliver, secure and maintain the site. Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts, which sends normal web request information to Google. We do not add website advertising, newsletter tracking or a separate analytics tracker. External links are governed by the linked service's privacy practices. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until it is overwritten or removed; you can delete it through Android app-storage controls. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request, maintain necessary support records and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact support@nomolabs.pro to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the same person, and legal obligations may limit deletion. We cannot recover deleted local saves or directly delete information independently held by Google; Google's retention and privacy controls are described in its policies.

Players under 13

The Last Caravan is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. Its target audience is separate from regional content ratings. If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information directly to us, contact support@nomolabs.pro so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Security and international services

We limit access to support information to people who need it for support and take reasonable steps to protect it. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Our hosting, email and advertising providers may process information in countries other than yours, under their respective privacy terms and applicable safeguards.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as game features, service providers or legal requirements change. We will revise the effective date and communicate material changes where required. For privacy or support questions, contact NomoLabs at support@nomolabs.pro.
