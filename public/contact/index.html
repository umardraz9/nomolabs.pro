BEFORE YOU WRITE

A few helpful answers.

Where can I find the games and apps?

Browse our collection for current product information. Product pages and store links will be added as each release is ready.

What should I include in a bug report?

The product name, device model, app version, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Can I suggest a feature or discuss a collaboration?

Yes. Email support@nomolabs.pro with a short description of your idea.
