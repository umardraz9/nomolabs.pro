A family chore app from NomoLabs

Super Tasker

Small tasks. Big adventures.

Turn everyday chores into missions. Parents set the tasks, kids show what they’ve finished, and everyone can see the progress.

See how it works ↓

From first chore to first reward

  1. Set up your family

    Sign in with Google as a parent. Create a child invite, then use the family code on your child’s device to set up their profile and PIN.

  2. Assign a mission

    Choose a preset chore or write your own. Set coin and XP rewards, schedule repeat chores and choose whether you need photo proof.

  3. Review and reward

    Kids submit their work for review. Approve the mission or ask for a touch-up. Approved missions earn coins kids can spend on family rewards you set.

Give kids a clear next step

The child’s dashboard brings today’s missions, routines and earned coins together. A morning routine can cover making the bed and packing a school bag. In the Rewards Vault, kids can work toward something they choose, like picking the next family movie.

See how routines are going

Open Insights from the parent dashboard to see weekly, monthly or yearly progress. Filter by child and switch between approved missions, XP and coins earned.

Family Plan

Eligible families can start a one-month trial without entering a payment method. The trial never starts a paid subscription automatically.

When subscriptions are available, the primary parent can choose a monthly or yearly plan for the family through Google Play. Google Play shows the price for your region before purchase.

Help with Super Tasker

Need help with a family code, sign-in or your account? Email support@nomolabs.pro.

Can parents choose the rewards?

Yes. Parents set the reward and coin cost. Kids can request a reward when they have saved enough coins.

Who can see mission photos?

Mission photo proofs are available to authorized parents in the family. Parents can choose the photo requirement when assigning a mission.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open Google Play Subscriptions with the Google account used for purchase.
