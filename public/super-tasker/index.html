YOUR FAMILY’S NEXT CHAPTER

A mission to finish.
A little win to celebrate.

Parents guide the journey. Kids take the next step. Super Tasker brings chores, progress and family rewards together.

01Bring the family together

Sign in with Google as a parent. Invite your child with a family code and set up their profile and PIN. Add a co-parent to help manage the family.

02Choose the day’s missions

Pick ready-made tasks or write your own. Set coin and XP rewards, choose photo requirements, and schedule recurring chores around your family’s routine.

03Show the work

Kids open their mission, finish the chore and submit it for review. Parents can request one photo or before-and-after proof when assigning a mission.

04Review, reward, repeat

Approve a mission or ask for a touch-up. Kids earn coins for approved work and can request a reward you set, like choosing a movie or planning a family picnic.

Who chooses the rewards?

Parents create the reward and set its coin cost. Kids can request it once they have enough coins.

Who can see mission photos?

Authorized parents in the family can review mission proof. Proof photos are kept out of public league boards.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage renewals through Google Play Subscriptions using the account that made the purchase. Deleting your Super Tasker account does not cancel the subscription.

How can I delete my account?

Use the in-app account deletion controls or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
